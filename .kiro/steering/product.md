# Product goals

CarrySync Kit: One Foundation. Two Applications. Zero Network Required for Data Exchange.

The team targets Kiroween 2026 Skeleton Crew with one reusable offline-first SDK and two independent apps: FieldLedger (inventory movements/history) and QuizRelay (quiz publication/answers). Exchange small structured event batches by QR, without a server, internet, Bluetooth pairing or Wi-Fi during transfer.

Day 1 is a session-only technical proof of concept. Durable storage, installed PWA behavior, camera acquisition and physical phone verification remain future work. Prefer a small reliable flow over feature breadth. Never claim full CRDT/application-state convergence from event-set tests.
